# World

## Birthday slice

Start at **Sunmeadow Stable**, an old countryside stable accessed through a mysterious invitation. Connect it to a compact riding area and one small section of **Evervale Village**. Include a few villagers, recurring cats, a small personal stable area, and a readable race route. One gradual Echo mystery ties horse, place, and memory together.

## Later regions

1. Sunmeadow Stable
2. Evervale Village
3. Windbrook Fields
4. Whisperwood Forest
5. Silvermist Lake
6. Starfall Ridge
7. Moonglade

These are long-term destinations, not an October 1 commitment. Unlock new space only after the slice is cohesive.

## World rules

The ordinary world stays recognizable and cozy. Magic appears as uncommon glowing trails, Echo fragments, unusual flowers, fog, hidden clearings, ancient horse marks, mysterious lights, or rare enchanted tack. Add cats as incidental world life and optional discoveries; no complex pet system is needed.
