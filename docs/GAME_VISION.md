# Game vision

**Whispers of Evervale** is an original 2D pixel-art countryside horse adventure and birthday gift for Hana. It should work as a charming, playable game first; personal details begin as quiet coincidences and become clear in a funny, warm birthday finale.

## Player promise

Arrive at an old stable, make a character, choose a horse, ride around a welcoming countryside, meet its people and cats, style horse and rider, decorate a small stable space, and uncover a gentle mystery. Horse and character customization lead the priorities, followed by decoration, racing, and exploration. Dialogue supports play rather than replacing it.

## Tone and scope

Cozy, funny, charming, and slightly magical. The ordinary countryside remains grounded; Echoes are rare memory fragments tied to horses, riders, and places. No combat is needed. The October 1 build is a stable vertical slice, not a complete world: polish a small Sunmeadow area, village corner, and one race before adding regions.

## Design guardrails

- Make preference matter more than optimal stats; keep racing casual and forgiving.
- Favor fewer finished choices over broad unfinished systems.
- No breeding, multiplayer, accounts, backend, or elaborate free-placement housing before the birthday build.
- Keep the player free to roam after the finale.
- Inspiration may inform readability and genre appeal, never copied characters, maps, assets, music, names, quests, or UI.
