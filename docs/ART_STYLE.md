# Art style

## Direction

High-quality, readable 2D pixel art with detailed countryside environments, expressive mid-proportion characters, recognizable horses, visible outfit changes, and practical animation. The target balances Terraria-like environmental detail with horse-game warmth; all designs must be original.

## Palette and atmosphere

Use teal, blue-green, muted turquoise, forest greens, warm wood browns, cream, and restrained gold. Natural wood and countryside tones ground the world; teal highlights Echo effects, selected UI states, and special birthday moments. Avoid a bright-blue UI wash.

Build atmosphere with layered backgrounds, warm lighting, subtle glow, drifting particles, animated foliage, water shimmer, shifting light, fog, fireflies, and weather where affordable. Keep effects tasteful and readable. Stability and playability outrank shader complexity.

## Characters and interface

Characters sit between Star Stable appeal and mild anime stylization: neither chibi nor realistic. Layered sprites should make hair, clothing, color, and accessories legible. Interface uses clear hierarchy, cream text, wood/countryside surfaces, and teal accents. Keep desktop controls and text comfortable to read.
